## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, tactile simplicity, and grounded authenticity of a home kitchen rooted in plant-based culinary traditions. Rather than adopting sterile, clinical aesthetics common to algorithmic wellness software, it draws from the tactile warmth of handcrafted culinary journals, sun-drenched countertops, and fresh harvest botanicals. 

The aesthetic merges contemporary editorial minimalism with rustic organic tactility:
- **Tone:** Grounded, familial, nourishing, and unhurried. It speaks with the calm confidence of a seasoned kitchen companion.
- **Visual Stance:** Pristine cream and herb-tinted paper substrates establish soft contrast. Imagery takes center stage, favoring natural light, macro botanical textures, and raw culinary preparation over stylized or hyper-polished mockups.
- **Hierarchy:** High visual discipline. Screens feature a single primary action, strict left-aligned reading rhythm, and deliberate avoidance of generic drop shadows and saturated neon highlights.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the vegetable garden and natural paper stocks. Every chromatic tone serves an organic functional role rather than decorative excess.

- **Primary (`#25401F` / `#2F5233`):** Deep heritage moss. Serves as the primary operational color for key interactions, primary action buttons, active navigation states, and authoritative typography.
- **Secondary (`#2F5233`):** Balanced leafy foliage green used for brand motifs, structural emphasis, and solid component surfaces.
- **Tertiary & Accent (`#A63446`):** Vibrant Beetroot. Reserved strictly for singular highlight triggers (e.g., favorite/like states, primary upload actions, and urgent notifications). It must never exceed 1-2 instances per view.
- **Culinary Accents:**
  - `accent-turmeric` (`#D9A441`): Used for star ratings, contextual culinary awards, and recipe recommendation highlights.
  - `text-stem-gray` (`#6B6F63`): Muted vegetal tone for captions, placeholders, structural dividers, and metadata.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - `bg-herb-white` (`#F3F6EE`): The global backdrop, tinted with soft dried herbs to remove clinical blue-white glare.
  - `surface-paper` (`#FDFBF6`): Warm parchment for elevated sheets, cards, recipe sheets, and dialog surfaces.
- **Borders:** `border-sage-mist` (`#DCE3D5`) delivers tactile edge definition across components without relying on harsh dark outlines.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects editorial culinary publishing:

1. **Display & Section Headings (`Libre Caslon Text`):** Evokes classic print gastronomy, vintage recipe anthologies, and artisanal packaging. Reserved for high-impact titles, section anchors, and prominent numeric indicators (such as culinary metrics or nutritional indices).
2. **Body, Interface, & Labels (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered for exceptional legibility and comprehensive Vietnamese diacritical mark support. Ensures comfortable, fatigue-free long-form reading across recipe directions, ingredient manifests, and member discussions.

### Rules of Usage
- **Case Sensitivity:** Maintain standard sentence case across all headings, badges, and labels. Avoid uppercase capitalization (`UPPERCASE`) as it conflicts with the relaxed, domestic tone.
- **Reading Measure:** Restrict recipe instructions and narrative prose to a maximum line length of 72 characters.
- **Alignment:** Standard content, lists, and articles remain strictly left-aligned. Center alignment is restricted to standalone numeric metric displays and hero badge highlights.
- **Decorations:** Exclude decorative directional symbols (e.g., `→` or `>>`) from link and button text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a strict 8-point rhythmic scale (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`), balancing culinary imagery against structured ingredient lists.

### Structural Grids
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout bounded by a maximum width of `1120px` with `24px` gutters and variable side margins. Content-heavy sidebars or filters occupy 3–4 columns; feed items occupy 8–9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `16px` gutters and `24px` horizontal margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single primary column with `16px` fluid edge gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Component interior spacing conforms to `16px` on mobile screens and scales to `24px` on desktop layouts.
- Inter-section transitions maintain generous breathing room: `48px` on mobile, expanding to `64px` or `96px` across desktop pages to deliver an editorial, uncrowded reading experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on tactile, flat paper planes rather than artificial digital depth. Multi-level elevation hierarchies are expressed through subtle tonal transitions between `bg-herb-white` and `surface-paper`, paired with thin botanical borders.

### Depth Rules
- **Base Surfaces & Cards:** Zero shadow. Cards rely exclusively on a `1px solid border-sage-mist` border against `bg-herb-white` with a `surface-paper` ground.
- **No Card Hover Lift:** Cards must never employ vertical translation or shadow amplification on hover. Instead, interactive response is conveyed via subtle background tone shifting or border recoloring.
- **The Single Shadow Exception:** To retain maximum contrast and tactical utility, a single elevated shadow is permitted across the entire platform, reserved strictly for floating overlays (modals, bottom sheets, avatar dropdown menus, and floating action buttons):
  - `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(43, 42, 37, 0.12);`
- **Modal Backdrops:** Layered overlays utilize an organic charcoal scrim composed of `rgba(43, 42, 37, 0.40)` rather than synthetic black.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a refined soft-corner philosophy (`roundedness: 1`), counterbalanced by an asymmetric corner radius that defines the platform's culinary identity.

### Corner Radius System
- **Standard Controls & Badges (0.25rem - 0.5rem / 4px - 8px):** Form fields, standard interactive buttons, micro-chips, and inline inputs use balanced `8px` radii.
- **Cards & Sheet Containers (0.75rem / 12px):** Standard list modules, recipe cards, and panel structures utilize a calm `12px` corner rounding.
- **Asymmetric Signature Corners:** Featured editorial recipe cards, banner frames, and hero culinary imagery feature a signature botanical cut: `top-left: 32px`, with the remaining corners resting at `8px` (`border-radius: 32px 8px 8px 8px`). This creates a distinctive silhouette reminiscent of an opened kitchen notebook.
- **Avatars & Pure Tokens:** Fully circular elements maintain `rounded-full` (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `primary-moss` (`#2F5233`), text in clean white (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` corner radius, minimum height of `44px`. On hover and active states, transitions smoothly to `primary-moss-hover` (`#25401F`) within 150ms. No embedded directional arrows.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border of `1.5px solid #2F5233`, text styled in `primary-moss`. Transitions to a faint wash of `rgba(47, 82, 51, 0.06)` on hover.
- **Destructive:** Border in `error-chili` (`#C1432E`), transparent ground, text in `error-chili`.
- **Floating Action Buttons (FAB):**
  - *Global Assistant FAB:* Grounded in `primary-moss` with white line-art icon, anchored to the viewport's bottom right.
  - *Contextual Creation FAB:* Anchored above the assistant FAB with an intentional `16px` vertical separation, styled in high-visibility `accent-beetroot` (`#A63446`).

### Category Markers & Badges
- Standard rounded pill tags are prohibited for food categories.
- Instead, categories utilize an editorial vertical accent spine: a `3px` left solid border bar paired with sentence-case text in `text-charcoal` (e.g., `▎ Món chính`). The spine rotates cyclically through `primary-moss`, `accent-turmeric`, `accent-beetroot`, and `text-stem-gray`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Bound by a `1px solid border-sage-mist` frame over a `surface-paper` fill, `8px` radius, with persistent sentence-case labels positioned strictly above the input boundary (never relying solely on placeholders). Focus states apply a crisp `2px solid primary-moss` ring with an offset of `2px`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` structural frame with `border-sage-mist` border. When checked, filled with `primary-moss` sporting a custom checkmark or solid center seed in white.

### Recipe & Article Cards
- **Feed Lists:** Structured as horizontal rows: an `8px` rounded square thumbnail anchored to the left, accompanied by typography, author metadata, and prep-time tags aligned to the right.
- **Pinned / Editorial Hero Cards:** Rendered with asymmetric corners (`32px 8px 8px 8px`), pristine `surface-paper` backgrounds, `1px solid border-sage-mist` outlines, and zero elevation shadow.

### Navigation & Menus
- **Public Header:** Fixed `64px` height, `surface-paper` substrate with a single baseline divider in `1px solid border-sage-mist`. Left-aligned brand typography in `Libre Caslon Text`. Active links are denoted by `primary-moss` coloring with an unbroken `2px` underline accent.
- **Weekly Schedule Tabs:** Single-row horizontal tabs utilizing crisp bottom-line border highlights for active selection instead of filled pill buttons, keeping layout noise minimal across all seven days.
- **Avatar Menu & Dropdowns:** `surface-paper` background with `1px solid border-sage-mist`, rounded `8px`, utilizing the platform's singular elevation shadow (`0 2px 12px rgba(43,42,37,0.12)`). Interactive item rows trigger a background shift to `bg-herb-white` on hover.

### Conversational & Assistant UI
- **User Message Nodes:** Backed by `primary-moss` fill with white text, right-aligned, styled with an organic asymmetric bubble radius (`12px 12px 2px 12px`).
- **AI Response Nodes:** Crisp `surface-paper` fill, bounded by `border-sage-mist`, left-aligned with a matching conversational radius (`12px 12px 12px 2px`).
- **Processing State:** Replaces conventional indeterminate circular spinners with a hand-drawn line animation illustrating a sprout unfurling from the ground.
